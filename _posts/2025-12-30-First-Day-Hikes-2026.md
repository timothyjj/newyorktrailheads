New York State's 15th annual First Day Hikes program features over 125 guided events on January 1, 2026, hosted by NYS Parks, DEC, and the Canal Corporation. Parking fees are waived at participating sites, and hikes range from easy family strolls to moderate winter treks. Below, we've compiled a few options within easy driving distance of those in the Capital Region.

## <a href="https://parks.ny.gov/parks/thacher" target="_blank">Thacher State Park</a>
**Location:** 830 Thacher Park Road, Voorheesville, NY.  
**Details:** Guided winter hikes at 10:00 a.m. and 1:00 p.m. from the main entrance. Family-friendly options on scenic trails along the escarpment with views of the Helderbergs.  
**Registration:** Required; sign up <a href="https://bit.ly/2026FirstDayHike" target="_blank">via this online form</a> or call 518-872-0800.

## <a href="https://parks.ny.gov/parks/saratoga" target="_blank">Saratoga Spa State Park</a>
**Location:** 1089 Route 9P, Saratoga Springs, NY.  
**Details:** Guided hike through forested paths and past historic mineral springs. Exact time TBD—contact park for updates.  
**Registration:** Call 518-584-2000 to confirm and register.

## <a href="https://parks.ny.gov/parks/moreau" target="_blank">Moreau Lake State Park</a>
**Location:** 605 Old Saratoga Road, Gansevoort, NY.  
**Details:** Family-friendly First Day Hike on lakefront and wooded trails. Event via official channels.  
**Registration:** Details on park's <a href="https://www.facebook.com/events/856138540396696/" target="_blank">Facebook event page</a> or call ahead.

## <a href="https://parks.ny.gov/parks/graftonlakes" target="_blank">Grafton Lakes State Park</a>
**Location:** 100 Grafton Lakes State Park Way, Cropseyville, NY.  
**Details:** Three options including a 1-mile easy loop, a 2.5-mile moderate hike, and a more difficult 3-mile hike to Dickinson Hill Fire Tower.  
**Registration:** Call 518-279-1155 to register and find more details on their <a href="https://www.facebook.com/GraftonLakesStatePark/posts/first-day-hikes-are-coming-upjoin-park-staff-to-ring-in-2026-on-a-guided-hike-th/1264374042386710/">Facebook page</a>.

## Tips for First Day Hikers
- **Gear Up:** Dress in layers for winter conditions; bring water, snacks, and microspikes if icy.  
- **Check Conditions:** Verify weather and trail status on official sites or by contacting park offices closer to January 1, 2026.  
- **More Options:** Browse the interactive statewide map for additional DEC lands and canal trails: <a href="https://parks.ny.gov/events/first-day-hikes">NYS Parks First Day Hikes</a> and <a href="https://dec.ny.gov/things-to-do/hiking/first-day-hikes">NYS DEC First Day Hikes</a>.
